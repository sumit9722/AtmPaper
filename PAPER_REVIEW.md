**I would not submit this as a completed research paper yet.** The main weaknesses are measurement validity and whether the results answer your research question.

I reviewed protocols, key scripts, datasets and results across the restarts. I did not find a finished manuscript, so this assessment concerns the research itself.

1. **Your strongest result does not answer the original question.** You wanted to establish whether environmental features improve prediction beyond surrounding crime. Restart 4’s AP improvement from **0.7195 to 0.8254** comes from adding **previous-year crime within 50 metres**. That establishes crime persistence. For an environmental contribution, compare **surrounding crime + prior local crime** against **those same predictors + environment**. Otherwise, the baseline omits the obvious strong predictor. [Results](restart_4_0/results/phase4/docs/03_results.md#training-only-feature-comparison)

2. **Your spatial precision exceeds what the measurements support.** Chicago and LA release block-level crime locations; LA explicitly supplies coordinates of the hundred block. Meanwhile, **48.4% of Chicago’s latest full-year observations use nonpoint OSM centroids**. A bank-building centroid is not necessarily the ATM’s location. Calculating distances precisely cannot establish that a crime actually happened within 25 or 50 metres of an ATM. This threatens the outcome labels themselves. [LA source documentation](https://data.lacity.org/Public-Safety/LAPD-NIBRS-Offenses-Dataset/k7nn-b2ep)

3. **The latest three-city comparison lacks a common primary baseline.** Chicago has all seven report-filtered crime predictors missing, including background crime. That is an entire city missing the main predictor family. Ordinary imputation would not restore comparable measurement. Separately, NYC retains “BURGLARY,TRUCK” while LA excludes vehicle burglary; **15 NYC site-year labels at 50 metres depend entirely on those truck cases**. Resolve the offense crosswalk before claiming comparable outcomes. [Missingness](restart6_0/results/phase3/docs/03_results.md) · [Codebook](restart6_0/data/processed/crime_codebook.csv)

4. **The large transfer gains need stronger controls.** In Restart 4, every small-city target-only M1 evaluation at 10% and 25% retained background alone. Those samples were too small for the feature-selection procedure to function adequately. Adding source data therefore also enabled feature selection. The comparison measures the benefit of the whole procedure, but does not isolate transfer of the same fixed model. Add fixed-feature controls before leaning on Buffalo’s **+0.396 AP** gain. [Selection logic](restart_4_0/scripts/common.py)

5. **Collecting more features is currently ahead of resolving the research design.** The expanded matrix has 116 predictors, including six pairs of exactly duplicated columns and three metropolitan totals that identify the cities perfectly within this dataset. Your 2,731 site-years represent **1,454 distinct OSM objects**, with repeated neighborhood measurements. More columns do not provide more independent evidence or a stronger explanation. [Expanded dataset](restart6_0/results/phase3_environment_expanded/README.md)

6. **The foundational review remains unfinished.** All 1,205 sampled linkage-review records and 632 facility-pair review records remain pending. Automated validation proves substantial computational consistency; it cannot establish physical facility identity, operational history or suitable crime-location accuracy. Repeatedly restarting also cannot make previously inspected years an untouched test set. And the older successful models concern different cities and a different ATM population from Restart 6.

**What you actually did well:** training-only preprocessing and selection, spatial separation, matched local-data budgets, and corrected multiple comparisons are implemented seriously. The audited Phase 4 metrics reproduce from saved predictions. You also preserve failed results and explicitly distinguish nearby crime from confirmed ATM attacks. Those are substantive strengths.

**My priority:** stop expanding features. Resolve spatial validity, facility identity and offense comparability; establish a usable common baseline; then test environmental improvement over a strong crime-history benchmark. A narrower exploratory paper could be defensible. A claim of generalizable ATM-specific environmental risk is currently unsupported.
