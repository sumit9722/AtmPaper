# Restart 5.0 — continuous-snapshot study specification, phases 1–2

Prepared 2026-10-19. Retrospective working specification, not a signed preregistration. Earlier 2024–2025 outcomes and model results have been inspected. This release performs data acquisition, exposure reconstruction, harmonization and descriptive linkage only. No model selection, fitting or significance target belongs in these phases.

## Question and population

Does the environment of bank-owned/in-bank ATM locations predict future nearby robbery or burglary beyond prior-year surrounding crime, and does this incremental association transfer between New York City, Buffalo and Rochester? Nearby police-recorded cases are not verified attacks on ATMs. The denominator is NYS DFS ndex-ad5r; its remit excludes independent non-bank ATMs. OSM-only candidates are not included. Use the archived Census municipal polygons, with their original provenance and a historical-boundary limitation.

## Time and the exposure gate

The user replaced the ten-year request before data building with the longest supported continuous ATM snapshot period. Recovered monthly DFS snapshots span 22 February 2023–15 January 2026. Analyze 2023–2025: 2023 contributes exact-identity partial exposure beginning no earlier than 22 February; 2024 and 2025 are the maximum complete calendar years bracketed by these snapshots. Use 2022–2025 crime, with 2022 baseline-only for partial 2023 rows. The earlier 2015–2025 acquisition is preserved as source material; 2015–2021 crime is not analyzed in this release. No ten-year panel is now requested. Exposure evidence must bracket each full outcome year. Public crime history does not imply historical ATM operation. Never backcast contemporary ATM locations, mark unavailable site-years zero, infer opening dates from publication times, or describe partial 2023 exposure as a full year.

Investigate the public revision index and older archive availability, preserve all successful and failed requests, and audit every required month. Inherit already acquired official snapshots only with original-byte checksums and explicit provenance. For supported years, require a unique city/normalized address in every one of the 14 monthly snapshots from prior December through following January, no adjacent gap over 62 days, and all coordinates within 50 m of the prior-December coordinate. Fix annual location to that coordinate. Do not merge missing/ambiguous addresses or select identities using crime. Save 10/25/50 m drift sensitivities. Monthly membership supports a continuity assumption, not known operating days.

Preserve exact-address/coordinate intervals as a separate strict sensitivity, including partial-year exposure. A missing month or identity breaks continuity. Count partial outcomes only inside supported half-open date intervals; store exact zero-day and excluded identities for audit. Empty historical coverage is missing evidence, not evidence that no ATMs existed. Never require multi-year survival for entry into an annual cohort. Actual opening/closing dates and operating days remain null.

## Harmonization and dates

Primary categories: robbery and burglary, including attempts where source-coded. Keep native offense/code/description, attempt flag, case ID, occurrence/report time, coordinates and source row reference. NYC extraction includes robbery/burglary only, with ATM-premises filtering prohibited. Buffalo includes all published categories and Rochester all Part I categories; publish the observed codebook, with other categories excluded from the endpoint. Do not compare broad theft across incompatible extracts.

Normalize Rochester epoch timestamps to America/New_York. Retain NYC/Buffalo local source times. Deduplicate globally within city and case ID across all acquired years; robbery > burglary > other, then later occurrence, then deterministic source-row order. Preserve every dropped row and conflicts in year, location, report date or category. Audit collisions for human review; this hierarchy is a working case-level construct, not a claim that multi-offense records are erroneous. Exclude missing case ID, invalid occurrence dates, invalid coordinates and points outside the source municipality, retaining reasons and source references. Unknown report times never qualify for a time-filtered baseline. A report date earlier than the occurrence date is anomalous and excluded from report-filtered counts, with unfiltered counts retained.

## Outcomes, backgrounds and geometry

Unit: exposure-supported site-year. Primary: any unique robbery/burglary case within 50 m during the outcome year. Retain counts, 25/100 m sensitivities, separate categories, and report-before-next-year counts. Annular baseline: prior-calendar-year cases strictly >200 m and <=500 m, reported before January 1 of outcome year, without invalid report chronology. Retain unfiltered, missing/invalid and late-report counts. Buffalo created_at is a source-ingestion proxy, not a verified report timestamp; explicitly flag it. Latest revisions do not recreate historical availability.

Use WGS84 ellipsoidal distances with conservative UTM candidate lookup (EPSG:32618 NYC; EPSG:32617 Buffalo/Rochester). Save every eligible event–site link, multiplicity at each radius and nearest-site sensitivity independently within each cohort/year. Preserve distinct addresses at nearby coordinates; provide <=10 m facility review pairs. For partial-year nearest-site sensitivity, rank only sites with supported exposure on the event date.

Quantify distance to municipal boundary and fraction of the 200–500 m annulus inside it, reporting boundary-excluded descriptive sensitivity. Quantify published coordinate rounding by city/year and the fraction of Buffalo links whose classification could change under a hypothetical half-cell rounding displacement. This is a sensitivity bound, not a correction or confidence interval. Do not treat geodesic arithmetic as restoring precision absent from public coordinates. Buffalo's 50 m endpoint remains unsuitable for confirmatory interpretation until better coordinates or an independently approved coarser design exists.

## Lessons and later-phase guardrails

Do not inherit a previous PASS as proof of current validity. Verify inputs and independently rebuild links/counts, including brute-force geodesic checks that can detect omitted candidates. Freeze protocol bytes before building. Maintain a nine-cell city/year availability matrix, a complete month grid, explicit attrition, reproducible review samples and unresolved gates. Separate computational validation, requested-period completion and independent scientific acceptance in machine-readable status.

Later phases require a new historically available feature dictionary. Prior-year nearby crime must be identified as temporal persistence, not an environmental mechanism. Separate a preregistered environmental increment from a crime-persistence increment; selecting whichever performs best after inspecting results changes the question. Use forward rolling-origin evaluation and spatial purges that account for both feature and outcome radii; prevent repeated-site leakage and fit preprocessing only in training. Preserve all source/target comparisons, matched target adaptation controls, multiple-testing correction and seed dependence. Neither high prevalence-driven AP nor a desired significant result is a stopping rule. Phase 3 onward is outside this request.
