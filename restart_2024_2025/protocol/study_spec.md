# 2024–2025 restart — phase 1 study specification

Prepared 17 October 2026. This is a retrospective working specification, not a preregistration: earlier project results were already inspected. Computational completion and independent research approval are separate statuses.

1. Question: does the environment of bank-owned/in-bank ATM sites predict nearby police-recorded robbery or burglary beyond prior-year surrounding crime, and does it transfer between New York City, Buffalo and Rochester? These are nearby incidents, not verified attacks on ATMs.
2. Population: NYS DFS bank-owned ATM archive ndex-ad5r. Assign cities by point-in-polygon using the archived Census municipal polygons. Exclude OSM-only candidates. Collapse exact normalized address/coordinate duplicates. Preserve distinct addresses and flag facilities within 10 m for review.
3. Period: 1 January 2024–31 December 2025, represented as two annual panels (366 and 365 days). Backgrounds use 2023 for the 2024 panel and 2024 for the 2025 panel. January 2026 ATM membership brackets December 2025 exposure. Crime from 2023 supplies baseline information only.
4. Exposure: preserve strict exact-identity monthly intervals and inventory as a sensitivity. The known 2024 coordinate revision yields no exact-identity full-year sites. The working cohort requires a city/normalized address present uniquely in every one of 14 snapshots (December of the prior year–January of the following year), with every coordinate within 50 m of its prior-December position. Fix location to that baseline. Do not select identities using crime outcomes. Save all linkage distances. Monthly membership implies continuity; actual operating days and opening/closing dates remain unknown. Apply the same address-continuity rule separately to each year for comparability; do not require membership in both years. Retain exact-identity cohorts for both years as a sensitivity. This cohort is exploratory until facility review.
5. Outcomes: unique city case identifiers with robbery or burglary, including attempts where classified by the source. Resolve multi-offense cases by robbery > burglary > larceny > other, then later event datetime; retain discarded rows. Primary outcome is any qualifying case within 50 m in each outcome year; retain counts and 25/100 m sensitivities, robbery/burglary components, multiplicity and nearest-cohort-site sensitivity. Main counts use the latest archived record of occurrence; separately retain report-before-the-following-year counts. No claim is made that the extract reconstructs a historical database vintage.
6. Background: Prior-year robbery/burglary at distances strictly >200 m and <=500 m. Primary baseline requires a nonmissing report date before 1 January of the outcome year; retain unfiltered, missing-report and late-report counts. Buffalo created_at is only a reporting-time proxy. Do not use broad theft as a primary outcome; classification is retained for audit only. NYC 2023 extraction covers only robbery/burglary, so a comparable broad-theft baseline is unavailable.
7. Geometry: WGS84 ellipsoidal final distances; candidate search in EPSG:32618 for NYC and EPSG:32617 for Buffalo/Rochester with 502 m search radius. Boundary clipping and current boundary vintage are limitations. Buffalo block coordinates can be coarser than 50 m and require separate precision review.
8. Later phases, not executed here: freeze historically available common environmental variables before fitting M0 logistic baseline, M1 regularized logistic plus environment, and M2 boosting. Use training-only preprocessing/tuning, spatially separated evaluation and an uninspected later outcome period. Primary metric AUPRC; secondary ROC-AUC, Brier, calibration and top-decile precision/recall. Working gain threshold 0.01 AUPRC with paired spatial-block 95% interval lower bound >0 (2,000 replicates), pending ratification. Transfer comparisons: local, each source, pooled sources, 10%/25% adaptation and matched local-only controls over 20 seeds. The two years permit a retrospective 2024-to-2025 comparison in later phases, but both years have already been inspected; they are not an untouched confirmatory test.
9. Acceptance: automated checks must reconcile source hashes/counts, dates, exposure, event links and panel counts. Independent offense, facility, linkage, boundary and precision review remains PENDING; confirmatory_eligible=false. Human approvals must never be inferred from automated checks.

Sources and acquisition provenance are retained in raw request sidecars and the generated source register. The restart reuses preserved official downloads; it does not claim fresh retrieval. Reproduction and exact outputs are documented in README.md and reports/execution.md.
